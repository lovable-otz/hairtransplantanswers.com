The procedure, simplified

How does a hair transplant work?

A same-day, outpatient procedure - here it is in three stages.

  1. 1

    Consultation & plan

    A surgeon assesses your donor area, agrees a graft count, and designs a natural hairline.

  2. 2

    The procedure

    Under local anaesthetic, follicles are harvested and implanted at the right angle and density.

  3. 3

    Recovery & growth

    You heal within days; transplanted hair sheds, then regrows over 6–12 months.

Quick answers

Hair transplant FAQs

Transplanted follicles are taken from areas resistant to balding, so they typically keep growing for life. Existing untreated hair can still thin, which is why planning for the future matters.

Most transplanted hair sheds within a few weeks, then regrows from around month three. Meaningful density usually appears by 6–9 months, with final results near 12 months.

With a skilled surgeon designing the hairline and angling grafts correctly, modern transplants are very difficult to detect. The clinic's real case photos are the best indicator of natural-looking work.

The surgery is done under local anaesthetic, so you're awake but shouldn't feel pain during it. Mild soreness, swelling, or tightness for a few days afterward is normal.
