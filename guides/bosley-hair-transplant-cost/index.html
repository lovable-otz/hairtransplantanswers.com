Bosley Hair Transplant Cost

In the US, most hair transplants cost between $4,000 and $15,000, driven mainly by the number of grafts, the technique (FUE or FUT), and the surgeon. Here's what shapes the price and how to get an accurate quote.

What affects the price?

Your total is driven mainly by the number of grafts, the technique (FUE or FUT), the surgeon's experience, and location. See the full cost breakdown and estimate your own with the calculator.

How do I get an accurate quote?

Pricing is set after a consultation that assesses your donor area and graft count. The quickest way to compare is to request a free quote from vetted providers.

What about brand-name providers?

Well-known brands perform standard FUE and FUT procedures, but quality and price still vary by individual surgeon. Judge any provider on surgeon experience, real case photos, and a transparent quote - see how to choose a clinic.

Is it worth the cost?

For suitable candidates, a transplant is a one-time cost for a permanent result - versus paying for treatments indefinitely. Compare the options in DIY vs professional.
