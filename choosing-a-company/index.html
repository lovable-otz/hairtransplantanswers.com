How to choose a hair transplant clinic you can trust

Look for a qualified surgeon leading the procedure, real unretouched case photos, transparent per-graft pricing, and a thorough consultation. This guide gives you the full checklist, the red flags to avoid, and the exact questions to ask.

The checklist

What should you look for in a hair transplant clinic?

Six things every reputable provider should offer - insist on all of them.

A qualified surgeon leads it

A board-certified doctor with hair-specific experience directs the procedure - not unsupervised technicians.

Real, unretouched cases

Genuine before-and-after photos of patients with hair loss similar to yours - not stock or over-edited images.

Transparent pricing

Clear per-graft or per-session pricing, a written quote, and no pressure to pay a same-day deposit.

Accreditation & standing

Membership of recognised bodies (e.g. the ISHRS) and a verifiable track record and reviews.

A thorough consultation

An honest assessment of your donor area, a realistic graft estimate, and a clear explanation of expected results.

Clear aftercare & follow-up

Written aftercare instructions and scheduled follow-ups to check your healing and growth over the first year.

Red flags

Warning signs of a clinic to avoid

If you spot any of these, slow down and get a second opinion.

Prices too good to be true

Deep discounts and "today only" package deals often mean corners cut on surgeon time or graft survival.

Technicians doing the surgery

If the surgeon only "supervises" while technicians perform most of the work, ask exactly who does what.

High-pressure sales

Pushy tactics, same-day deposits, and reluctance to let you compare quotes are all warning signs.

Vague or evasive answers

If they can't clearly explain your graft count, technique, or what's included, that's a reason to walk away.

How do I find reputable hair restoration surgeons near me?

Start with surgeons who are board-certified and members of recognised bodies like the ISHRS, then shortlist by verified case photos and reviews. Booking two or three consultations lets you compare assessments and quotes. We can also match you with vetted clinics in your area.

Who actually performs the surgery - the surgeon or technicians?

In many clinics, trained technicians assist with extraction and implantation under a surgeon's supervision. That's normal - but you should know exactly who does each step. Ask directly, and be cautious if the answer is vague or the surgeon is barely involved.

How do I compare hair restoration companies?

Compare on the things that drive results: surgeon experience, technique offered, honest graft estimates, transparent pricing, and real case photos - not just the headline price. A slightly higher quote from a proven surgeon often beats a cheap package. See how pricing works.

How should I read hair restoration reviews?

Look for detailed reviews with photos and timelines rather than one-line ratings. Weigh patterns over outliers, check multiple sources, and be wary of clinics with only perfect reviews or lots of complaints about pressure and results. Reviews support your research - they don't replace a consultation.
