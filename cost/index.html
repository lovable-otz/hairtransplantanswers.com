How much does a hair transplant cost?

In the United States, a hair transplant typically costs $4,000 to $15,000 - around $3 to $8 per graft. Your total depends on the number of grafts, the technique (FUE or FUT), the surgeon's experience, and where you have it done. Below, see costs broken down and estimate your own.

How much does a hair transplant cost in the US?

Most US hair transplants fall between $4,000 and $15,000. Smaller hairline work sits at the lower end; full restorations of the crown and mid-scalp reach the top. Because pricing is driven by graft count, the size of the area you're treating is the biggest single factor in your total.

Procedure sizeGraftsTypical US cost
Small (hairline / temples)500–1,500$4,000–$8,000
Medium (hairline + mid-scalp)1,500–2,500$8,000–$12,000
Large (full restoration)2,500–4,000+$12,000–$15,000+

How much does a hair transplant cost per graft?

US clinics usually charge $3 to $8 per graft. A 2,000-graft procedure at $5 per graft works out to about $10,000. Some surgeons price per session rather than per graft, so always ask which model a quote uses - it changes how two prices compare.

How much does an FUE hair transplant cost?

FUE (follicular unit extraction) typically costs more per graft than FUT because it's more labour-intensive and leaves no linear scar. Expect roughly $5–$8 per graft for FUE in the US. FUT can be $3–$6 per graft, which is why large cases are sometimes quoted lower with FUT. See the full FUE vs FUT comparison.

How much is a hair transplant in Turkey - and why is it cheaper?

Turkey is the world's most popular destination, with all-inclusive packages often $1,500–$3,500. Lower labour costs, high volume, and bundled travel drive the price down. Quality varies enormously, so vet the surgeon, not just the package - and factor travel, aftercare, and follow-up into any comparison.

How much does a men's or beard transplant cost?

Men's scalp transplants follow the ranges above. A beard or facial hair transplant usually costs $3,000–$7,000, depending on how many grafts are needed to reach the density you want. Eyebrow transplants are smaller and often $2,000–$4,000.

Can you finance a hair transplant?

Yes. Because insurance treats transplants as cosmetic, most clinics offer financing or monthly payment plans through medical lenders. Spreading a $10,000 procedure over 24–48 months is common. Always check the APR and total repayable, not just the monthly figure.
