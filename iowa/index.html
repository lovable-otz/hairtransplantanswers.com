How much does a hair transplant cost in Iowa?

In Iowa, most hair transplants fall between $4,000 and $13,000. The biggest factor is the number of grafts you need, followed by the technique (FUE or FUT) and the surgeon's experience. Prices in major metro areas tend to sit at the higher end. See the full national breakdown on the cost page.

How do I find a reputable clinic in Iowa?

Iowa has qualified surgeons, especially around its larger cities. Shortlist by surgeon experience, real unretouched case photos, and transparent per-graft pricing - and book two or three consultations before deciding. Use our clinic checklist, or let us match you with vetted local providers.

What can I expect from the procedure and recovery?

The procedure and recovery are the same wherever you have it: a same-day outpatient operation under local anaesthetic, followed by shedding and then new growth over 6–12 months. Read how it works and the aftercare guide.
